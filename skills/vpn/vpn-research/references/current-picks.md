# Current Free-VPN Picks — verified 2026-09-30

Re-verify if this file is older than ~6 months: caps, ownership, and
jurisdictions change. Re-run the checklist in `SKILL.md` on anything new.

## Recommendation order

1. **Proton VPN Free** — the default. Unlimited bandwidth, no ads, no-logs
   policy verified by annual Securitum audits (latest Sep 2025). Free server
   pool is auto-selected only (Quick Connect, ~10 locations per 2026 reviews).
   WireGuard/OpenVPN/IKEv2 + free Stealth obfuscation. 1 device. Proton AG,
   Switzerland (outside 14 Eyes; now under the non-profit Proton Foundation).
   All apps open source. Email signup, no card. Streaming blocked on free.
2. **Windscribe Free** — 10 GB/month (2 GB without confirmed email),
   unlimited devices, manual choice of 10 countries, unblocks some streaming.
   WireGuard/OpenVPN/IKEv2/Stealth/WStunnel; free R.O.B.E.R.T. ad/malware
   blocker. No-logs claim is **court/seizure-tested** (Greek case dismissed
   Apr 2025 for lack of logs; Dutch server seized Feb 2026, RAM-only design
   yielded nothing) but **no formal published no-logs audit** — say so
   honestly. Toronto, Canada (5 Eyes); independently owned, apps open source.
3. **hide.me Free** — unlimited data, 7–8 locations
   with manual choice, 1 device. Email signup required (as of 2026-09-30;
   the old no-signup flow is gone). No-logs verified by Securitum audit (2024).
   eVenture Ltd., Malaysia (outside all intel alliances). Slower on distant
   servers; no streaming/torrenting on free. Best zero-friction fallback.
4. **PrivadoVPN Free** — 10 GB/30 days at full speed, then 1 Mbps "emergency
   servers" instead of a hard cutoff. 13 locations, manual choice, P2P
   allowed, fastest free tier measured. **No independent audit published.**
   Moved HQ Switzerland → Iceland (effective May 2026) over Swiss
   surveillance-law proposals. Thin Linux support (manual config only).
5. **TunnelBear Free** — 2 GB/month, but 46+ countries, unlimited devices,
   audited annually by Cure53, VigilantBear kill switch, GhostBear
   obfuscation. McAfee-owned, US/Canada jurisdiction (5 Eyes). Essentially
   an audited trial — fine for occasional use.
